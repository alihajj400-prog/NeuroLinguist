# Data Access

NeuroLinguist is trained on the **DementiaBank Pitt Corpus**, a clinical dataset of
spontaneous speech recordings and transcripts from healthy controls and individuals
with dementia.

## Why the data isn't in this repository

The Pitt Corpus is **access-restricted** under a data-use agreement administered by
TalkBank/DementiaBank. Redistributing the audio or transcripts — even a subset — would
violate that agreement. For this reason, the `data/` directory is **not** included in
this repository, and the raw `.cha`, `.wav`, and `.mp3` files are excluded via
`.gitignore`.

## How to obtain access

1. Visit **https://dementia.talkbank.org/**
2. Follow TalkBank's membership/registration process to request access to the Pitt Corpus.
3. Once approved, download the Control and Dementia recordings and CHAT (`.cha`) transcripts.

## Expected directory layout

After obtaining the data, arrange it as follows so the pipeline scripts can find it:

```
data/
├── audio/
│   ├── Control/{cookie,fluency,recall,sentence}/      # .wav files
│   └── Dementia/{cookie,fluency,recall,sentence}/
├── raw/
│   ├── Control/{cookie,fluency,recall,sentence}/      # .cha transcripts
│   └── Dementia/{cookie,fluency,recall,sentence}/
├── transcripts/      # generated by preprocess_transcripts/
├── features/         # generated by feature_extraction/
└── metadata/
    └── metadata.csv  # generated by scripts/build_metadata.py
```

The helper scripts in `scripts/setup/` (`createfolders.py`, `organize_files.py`,
`build_metadata.py`, etc.) assist with downloading, organizing, and indexing the corpus
once you have access.
